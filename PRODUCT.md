# SideQuest

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. A hackathon landing page that has to open anywhere, instantly.

## Users

College students living in one dorm or belonging to one club. They need small things on short notice (a tripod tonight, a charger for an afternoon, someone to grab a print order) and have small things to give (gear to lend, errands along their route, skills like debugging Python). Often broke. Tired of asking five group chats.

Secondary: hackathon judges evaluating it as a multi-agent demo.

## Product Purpose

Each student has an AI agent. You text it what you need and what you can offer. Agents negotiate with other students' agents to arrange a trade of favors, including multi-party chains when no direct swap exists. Success: everyone in a chain gets what they need, handoffs fit their schedules, nobody had to ask awkwardly.

## Positioning

Three-way favor chains. Nobody has a direct match; agents discover a cycle (A lends B, B helps C, C lends A), negotiate borrowing windows and handoffs, and send each person one proposal to accept. Private schedules stay private; only relevant offers and availability are shared.

## Operating Context

- Lives in a Discord server for one dorm or club; students message a bot.
- Agents negotiate constraints: return-by times ("tripod back before my 8 a.m."), routes ("only pickups on my way"), class schedules.
- Everyone confirms before a chain activates. If anyone declines, agents find another arrangement.
- Prototype scope: three student agents, small inventory of offers and requests, a visual of the chain forming. Borrowing items and simple errands only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Prototype / demo stage. No user counts, no real usage data.
- Terminology: favors, trades, chains, handoffs, borrowing windows, proposals.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: SideQuest.
- Pitch line: "Your campus already has everything you need. Your agent finds the trade that makes everyone say yes."
- Voice: casual, student-to-student, a bit funny, never corporate.
- User-pinned aesthetic: minimalist, light colors, no gradients, heavy expressive animation, feels human-made.

## Evidence on Hand

Only the example scenario (You / Jordan / Sam: charger, print pickup, tripod). No testimonials, no metrics, no logos. Do not fabricate any; demo content is labeled as an example.

## Product Principles

1. Everyone says yes or nothing happens.
2. Share the offer, never the calendar.
3. One message per person, not a group-chat scramble.
4. Small favors, real stakes: times and returns are honored.
