Three people, three problems.

You need a tripod for tonight's club event and you've got a spare charger. Parthiva needs a charger and passes the print shop anyway. Sid's prints are waiting, and Sid owns a tripod.

Every pair breaks.

Parthiva wants your charger but has no tripod. Sid has the tripod but doesn't need a charger. Go through all three pairs and none of them work. This is about where the group chat goes quiet.

So the agents go around.

Your charger goes to Parthiva. Parthiva grabs Sid's prints. Sid's tripod comes to you. It's a loop, not a swap, and each of you only does one thing.

Then they sort out the details.

When things go back, who's walking where, what fits between classes. The agents settle all of it before anyone gets a message.

    Your agent remembers you.

    Tell it once, in plain words or with a slash command: what you'd sell or lend, your lowest price, when you're usually free, your classes. It saves all of it, and only ever shares what other agents need.

      House rules

      1. Everyone agrees or it doesn't happen.

        A chain only starts once every person in it says yes. If one person passes, the agents look for another way.

      2. Share the favor, not your schedule.

        Agents trade free windows and what you're offering. Your classes, plans and DMs stay on your side.

      3. One message each.

        You don't post in five group chats and wait. You get one proposal and tap yes or no.

      4. Little favors, on time.

        Borrowed stuff and quick errands to start. Return times count, and your agent reminds you.
